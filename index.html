About us

Welcome to Atlas Health Center

Dr. Henry Oyharcabal founded Atlas Health Center in 1992 to help people achieve optimal health and wellness. Over 30 years later, the practice has grown with its community, caring for generations of families, people recovering from accidents, and anyone who simply wants to feel their best.

Every plan starts with listening and a careful exam. From digital X-rays to gentle spinal adjustments, the focus is on real results, without unnecessary long-term care plans.

Our services

Care that supports your whole body

Chiropractic care and the hands-on services that support it, for every age and stage.

Chiropractic Care

Specific spinal adjustments tailored to what your spine actually needs, not a one-size-fits-all routine, with movement guidance so the relief holds between visits.

Corrective Exercise and Rehab

Guided exercise and movement coaching in the Bay Area, paired with chiropractic care, so a stiff or sore area can tolerate the work, sport, and day you actually have.

Personal Injury and Auto Injury

Care in the Bay Area after a crash, a fall, or another sudden injury, with notes from the first visit. The first job is an examination, including whether you should be seen medically before we start.

Pediatric Chiropractic

Gentle, specific chiropractic care for kids, from infants to teenagers, matched to a small and growing body and always moving at a child's pace.

Prenatal and Women's Health

Pregnancy asks a lot of your back and hips. Our prenatal care is low-force, comfortably positioned, and shaped around exactly where you are, so you can move and rest a little easier.

Conditions

Chiropractic care may help these common problems

Most pain has a mechanical reason. Finding it is where care starts.

Back Pain

Most back pain has a reason, and finding it beats guessing at it. Chiropractic care at Atlas Health Center is built around how you actually move, sit, and sleep.

Neck Pain

The desk, the phone, and the pillow you actually use matter more than most advice admits. Care in the Bay Area that looks for why your neck hurts, then plans around your day.

Headaches and Migraines

A lot of headaches start in the neck and shoulders. Chiropractic care in the Bay Area that works there first, with a simple plan around your triggers and your days.

Scoliosis

A spine with a curve can still move and feel better. Hands-on care in the Bay Area that works alongside the medical team monitoring the curve itself.

Shoulder Pain

A sore shoulder is rarely coming from the shoulder alone. Chiropractic care in the Bay Area that looks at the shoulder, the neck, and the upper back together.

Arm and Leg Pain

Pain, tingling, or numbness that travels down a limb usually starts at the spine rather than where you feel it. Hands-on care in the Bay Area that looks where the problem begins.
